About & data sources

This is an exploratory project. Fire detections are satellite hotspots, not confirmed wildfires, and not the number of distinct fires. Neither layer is a validated wildfire prediction. NASA advises against using these data for tactical decision-making or for conditions at a local scale. Do not use this map for operational or emergency decisions.

Layers

  • Fire detections: VIIRS Suomi NPP, wildfire-type detections, 2015-10 to 2025-10.
  • Layer A, historical detection likelihood: where detections have been more frequent (driven by low elevation and nearness to buildings). Reflects human presence as much as vegetation.
  • Layer B, fuel and terrain susceptibility: expert-scored from land cover and slope. Exploratory and unvalidated; it does not match the detections.
